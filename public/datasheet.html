DATASHEET & INSTRUCTION MANUAL — v3.26

1. SPECIFICATIONS

BOXDRIVE is a profoundly capable closed-loop stepper controller engineered for silence, absolute precision, and wireless freedom.

  • Microcontroller ESP32-C6 Mini
  • Motor Driver TMC2209 (StealthChop & SpreadCycle)
  • Position Encoder AS5600 Magnetic (12-bit)
  • CAN Transceiver SN65HVD230D
  • Connectors 2x XT30PW_2_2 in/out (Daisy-Chain)
  • Operating Voltage 4.75 V – 24.0 V
  • Max Current 2.0 A RMS

2. PIN CONFIGURATION

Internal ESP32-C6 GPIO assignments for all on-board peripherals.

GPIO SIGNAL DESCRIPTION
GPIO 0 DIR TMC2209 Direction control
GPIO 1 STEP TMC2209 Step pulse signal
GPIO 2 EN TMC2209 Enable (Active LOW)
GPIO 3 DIAG TMC2209 StallGuard / Step loss detection
GPIO 4 PWM General purpose PWM output pin
GPIO 14 UART RX TMC2209 UART Receive
GPIO 18 UART TX TMC2209 UART Transmit
GPIO 19 LED On-board Status Indicator LED
GPIO 20 CANTX SN65HVD230D CAN Transmit
GPIO 21 CANRX SN65HVD230D CAN Receive
GPIO 22 SCL AS5600 I2C Clock
GPIO 23 SDA AS5600 I2C Data
